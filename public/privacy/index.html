Fawkes Mobile Privacy Policy

Fawkes Mobile uses phone numbers, message content, timestamps, and delivery status only to provide requested alerts and conversational messages.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Mobile information and text-message consent are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Service providers such as Twilio may process information only as needed to deliver messages.
