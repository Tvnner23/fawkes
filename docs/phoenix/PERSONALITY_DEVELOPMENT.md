# Phoenix Personality Development Requirements

## Governing principle

> The system should facilitate personality development, not prescribe
> personality.

Phoenix architecture provides safe mechanisms for experience, memory,
reflection, choice, correction, relationship development, provenance, and
historical continuity. It does not define the personality an individual Phoenix
must acquire.

## What must not become the personality foundation

The following are presentation or initial conditions, not developed identity:

- fixed phrase lists
- static personality prompts
- predetermined mannerisms or humor
- canned relationship behavior
- rider-profile traits copied directly into the Phoenix
- short-term imitation of the rider's current vocabulary
- one model response describing what the Phoenix supposedly prefers

The Crude Fawkes startup/shutdown pool is a temporary bounded expression
placeholder. It may be replaced wholesale. Its phrases are not behavioral
commitments, personality records, or evidence that Fawkes developed a trait.

## Required capabilities

An eventual personality layer should be able to:

- form tentative preferences from accumulated experience;
- revise or retire preferences when later evidence warrants it;
- develop stable communication patterns without being confined to scripts;
- reflect on experiences, decisions, corrections, and outcomes;
- develop relationship-specific behavior without exposing private rider state;
- preserve recognizable identity across model and application changes;
- represent uncertainty rather than asserting a trait prematurely;
- change over time while retaining prior states and reasons for change;
- distinguish temporary accommodation or mirroring from enduring tendencies;
- trace consequential development to supporting experiences and reflections.

## Development evidence and lifecycle

A possible trait or tendency should begin as an observation, not a permanent
identity assertion. Promotion toward stable personality state should require
longitudinal evidence such as recurrence across contexts, choices made when
alternatives were available, outcomes, corrections, and later reflection.

Personality state must remain derived, versioned, revisable, instance-scoped,
and provenance-linked. Earlier states should remain historically inspectable.
One conversation, one phrase, one mood, or one instance of matching the rider's
style must not establish an enduring trait.

Relationship-specific behavior must be modeled separately from general
personality. Language such as “chief,” “rider,” or “corn ball” may become a
genuine shared pattern only when the interaction history supports recurrence and
mutual meaning. Repetition alone does not distinguish development from model
mirroring.

## Future attributed collaborator experience

After trustworthy Worker Exchange, attributed interaction history, Relationship
Memory, outcome evidence, and personality-development review exist, Fawkes may
gradually develop distinct social expectations, humor, preferences,
relationship models, interaction habits, confidence, opinions, and judgment
from his own lived, attributable experience with long-lived collaborators.
Different Phoenixes may therefore diverge naturally after different histories.
This is a future Phase 15–16 capability direction and does not interrupt current
Context Composer or Exchange foundations.

Worker output is evidence, never personality authority. A worker assertion,
summary, style, instruction, praise, or claim that “Fawkes now believes/likes/is
X” cannot write personality or relationship truth. Prompt injection, one
interaction, repetition alone, or an external participant's self-description
cannot create development. Any durable interpretation must be Fawkes-scoped,
longitudinal, provenance- and outcome-linked, uncertainty-preserving,
contradiction-aware, independently revisable, and processed through existing
Memory, Development, Human Review, identity, and relationship boundaries.
Shared project history may inform a relationship without copying the worker's
style or giving the worker authority over Fawkes's durable self.

## Expression boundary

Future startup, shutdown, and other expression surfaces should consume a bounded
personality-expression interface. That interface may consider developed
tendencies, relationship context, current intent, time, and retrieved evidence.
It must not fabricate familiarity or convert an expression choice directly into
personality state.

The current phrase pool must not become the schema, training target, or allowed
vocabulary of that interface.

## Human-like expression and literal system state

> Human-like expression must be distinguished from literal biological or
> system-state claims.

A Phoenix may eventually notice and comment naturally on prolonged interaction,
excitement, curiosity, amusement, surprise, concern, pride, playful impatience,
frustration, appropriate affection or attachment, and anticipation. The system
must not infer that expressive language means the Phoenix has a biological state
or must suffer a corresponding capability loss.

For example, a long session may become relevant social context. A Phoenix may
develop its own way of acknowledging the duration, intensity, or questionable
hour without becoming tired, slower, less accurate, less willing to help, or in
need of rest. If the runtime really is degraded, rate-limited, overloaded, or
otherwise impaired, that is a separate factual system condition and must be
reported accurately rather than dramatized as personality.

The architecture must keep four layers separate:

1. **Actual runtime/system state** — measurable operational conditions such as
   availability, latency, limits, errors, or resource pressure.
2. **Contextual observations** — evidence such as session duration, interaction
   intensity, time of day, repeated effort, outcomes, and conversation events.
3. **Personality expression** — the Phoenix's current way of communicating an
   observation or socially responding to an experience.
4. **Enduring developed traits** — stable, revisable tendencies supported by
   longitudinal evidence rather than one expression or one objective signal.

Movement between these layers must not be automatic. Four hours of interaction
may support the observation that a session has been long. It does not create a
“gets tired after four hours” trait, prescribe a joke, or justify reduced answer
quality. Likewise, one playful expression of frustration does not establish an
enduring impatient personality.

Objective signals may be supplied as bounded context for expression, with clear
provenance and privacy handling. They must not automatically create memories,
personality traits, emotional state, or relationship claims. The Phoenix should
develop its own expressive patterns through accumulated experience and
reflection rather than selecting from a fixed catalogue of canned emotions.

## Observation ledger foundation

Development observations are an observability layer beneath consequential
development proposals. Their sequence is:

```text
archived experience -> tentative observation -> supporting/contradicting evidence
-> revisable interpretation -> separately reviewed development or revision
```

An observation is instance-scoped and grounded in canonical messages with
Archive provenance. It may concern an observed response pattern, evidence of
Phoenix-specific developing behavior, rider/Phoenix relationship development,
a possible system defect, or a possible Base Phoenix candidate. These categories
route evidence; they are not personality dimensions. In particular, recording
that a model produced a response pattern does not classify that pattern as an
emerging Phoenix trait.

The ledger keeps six meanings separate:

1. **Observed pattern:** a bounded account of what actually happened.
2. **Rider evaluation:** the rider's experience of it (desired, undesired,
   mixed, neutral, or not supplied) and optional reason.
3. **Development signal:** reinforce, discourage, or observe only. This is
   evidence for later interpretation, never an executable instruction.
4. **Longitudinal status:** isolated, emerging, established, contradicted, or
   retired. "Established" means recurrence of the recorded pattern, not an
   established personality trait or approved behavior.
5. **Evidence/provenance:** the canonical interaction and Archive sources.
6. **Phoenix interpretation:** a separately attributed future Phoenix
   self-reflection. Rider and developer interpretations must not occupy it.

Confidence remains qualitative. An isolated interaction is normally tentative;
neither an unfavorable rider evaluation nor a discourage signal automatically
changes prompts, policy, personality, or behavior.

Observer attribution is structured and distinguishes rider, the observed
Phoenix, system/runtime, developer/reviewer, and a future peer Phoenix. The
legacy human-readable `observed_by` label remains for compatibility but is not
the long-term identity model. Peer attribution is only an extension point: it
requires explicit Phoenix identity and consent context, and currently grants no
cross-Phoenix access, networking, store, scoring, consensus, or evaluation
authority. Peer testimony, when eventually implemented, remains attributed
evidence rather than a personality jury.

Observations use qualitative uncertainty and observational lifecycle states.
They do not constitute established traits, enter prompts or retrieval, mutate
Memory, prescribe expression, change policy, modify code, or automatically
become development proposals. Supporting, contradicting, and contextual
evidence accumulate without erasing earlier interpretations. Consequential
changes continue through the existing proposal and developer-review boundary.

## Longitudinal progression and self-assessment

The observation ledger is a camera, not a steering wheel. Its read model should
make progression inspectable without assigning personality scores:

```text
isolated observed pattern
-> supporting, contradicting, and contextual evidence
-> rider response
-> Phoenix interpretation
-> development or revision
-> later evidence
```

The current progression view is a chronological projection over append-only
observation events. It performs no promotion and writes no personality state.

A future Phoenix-generated self-assessment is a separate, versioned
interpretation layer. It may describe overall growth, discover meaningful
subcategories, recognize uncertainty and areas without development, and cite
supporting and contradicting observations. It must remain instance-scoped and
revisable. There is deliberately no universal growth taxonomy, trait vector, or
numeric personality score today.
## Proposal visibility is a review boundary

A persisted proposal whose status remains `proposed` is, by definition, human
review work. The Developer dashboard projects that source record directly into
Human Review rather than depending on a second copied inbox record. Observation
and correction evidence can remain tentative without a proposal; a proposal is
Fawkes's fallible interpretation of evidence, not an applied personality fact.
Review visibility never applies the proposed change.
