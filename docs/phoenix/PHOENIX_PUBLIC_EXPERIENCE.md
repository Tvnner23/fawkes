# Phoenix Public Experience

**Direction ID:** `phoenix.public_experience`  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Canonical product direction; implementation not started

## Purpose and ownership

This document owns the durable product direction for Phoenix's permanent public
home: its audience promise, the public companion's identity boundary, truthful
public maturity, voluntary support, and progression into authenticated Phoenix
access.

It does not own the authenticated client, Phoenix identity, presentation state,
manifestation asset format, capability authority, private evidence, recovery,
payments, providers, hosting, or final visual design. Existing owners remain:

- [App Architecture](APP_ARCHITECTURE.md) for the authenticated durable client
  and future instance resolution;
- [Presentation Architecture](PRESENTATION_ARCHITECTURE.md) for validated,
  declarative Phoenix presentation and stable rider controls;
- [Presence Asset Contract](PRESENCE_ASSET_CONTRACT.md) and
  [Embodiment Development Gates](EMBODIMENT_DEVELOPMENT_GATES.md) for Fawkes
  manifestation assets, evidence, and promotion gates;
- [Capability Architecture](CAPABILITY_ARCHITECTURE.md) for capability
  negotiation and authority;
- [Phase 0 Trust Foundation](PHASE0_TRUST_FOUNDATION.md) and
  [Checkpoint and Recovery](PHOENIX_CHECKPOINT_RECOVERY.md) for isolation,
  privacy, integrity, and recovery;
- Development, Assurance, and Human Review for evidence, verification, and
  approval; and
- [Foundation](FOUNDATION.md) for provider independence, commercial
  optionality, cost/quality discipline, and Phoenix identity invariants.

Examples are illustrative unless marked `MUST`, `MUST NOT`, `SHOULD`, or `MAY`.
They do not freeze a framework, vendor, model, renderer, animation list, name,
layout, route catalogue, or public copy.

## Permanent public home

The website is intended to become the permanent public home of Phoenix and the
primary destination for curious visitors, supporters, technical audiences,
press, partners, and future riders. Permanence applies to the domain, product
identity, primary URLs, information architecture, public-content model, design
language, and audience relationship. It does not require the first framework,
runtime, deployment, renderer, or provider to survive forever.

One coherent destination MUST NOT be interpreted as one monolithic application,
deployment, technology stack, or trust boundary. Public content, the public
companion, hosted support, and future authenticated Phoenix access may evolve
independently behind stable product contracts.

This is an approved parallel Rider Experience/product track. It is not a
prerequisite for unrelated Phoenix Core work, and Fawkes Alpha is not a
prerequisite for the first serious public website.

## Four selling systems

Public v0.1 is organized around four customer-facing systems:

1. **Spectacle:** a genuinely impressive working embodied public companion.
2. **Dream:** an ambitious account of what Phoenix is intended to become.
3. **Proof:** selected real Fawkes and engineering evidence showing that the
   project is being built.
4. **Money:** a tasteful, low-friction way to support development voluntarily.

Security, privacy, accessibility, performance, deployment, analytics, abuse and
cost controls, payment isolation, evidence review, and graceful fallback are
required quality infrastructure beneath those systems. They are not separate
public products.

The intended non-manipulative progression is:

```text
wonder -> response -> understanding -> desire -> credibility -> optional support
```

The site MAY sell ambition, possibility, emotional appeal, production value,
scale, and intended experience forcefully. It MUST NOT use dark patterns, false
scarcity, deceptive countdowns, guilt, parasocial financial pressure, false
capability claims, or fabricated access and delivery promises.

## Public site companion

The public companion is a primary selling feature, not a corner chatbot or
secondary documentation assistant. It exists to demonstrate why embodied AI,
environmental awareness, responsiveness, and composed character performance can
feel different from ordinary chat.

The companion SHOULD visibly inhabit the site, respond to visitor activity,
direct meaningful gaze and attention, idle naturally, converse where useful,
react physically, perform authored signature moments, and guide a visitor
through a small number of installed experiences. Richness SHOULD come from
composition, contextual reuse, parameterized authored movement, session-local
callbacks, timing, asymmetry, restraint, and layered deterministic/authored/model
behavior—not raw capability count.

A narrow system with approximately five to ten excellent behavior families and
a few exceptional known environmental anchors is preferable to broad mediocre
locomotion or hundreds of shallow reactions. Exact behaviors remain an
implementation hypothesis until a later companion acceptance contract is
approved.

### Not a Phoenix individual

The public companion is categorically not Fawkes and not a Phoenix individual.
It has no lifelong continuity, Phoenix Memory, lived Phoenix developmental
history, private rider relationship, Worker Exchange authority, private tools,
Fawkes Archive, or access to Tanner's Phoenix. It has no obligation to use the
complete Phoenix architecture.

It MAY use session-local context, curated public knowledge, deterministic
behavior, authored animation, prewritten or cached responses, lightweight model
inference, semantic website awareness, and tightly installed actions. These are
desirable product shortcuts when they strengthen the experience without
misrepresenting its nature.

The experience SHOULD disclose the distinction once, clearly and elegantly,
without surrounding every interaction with warnings. When asked directly, the
companion MUST answer truthfully and naturally that it is a public
demonstration/guide rather than a persistent Phoenix, and it SHOULD identify
Fawkes as the first Phoenix being built.

Public companion interactions MUST NOT become Fawkes's Memory, autobiographical
history, relationship evidence, personality, identity, or capability truth.
Aggregate product research derived from public use requires its own privacy and
evidence review.

## Controlled revelation and truthful maturity

Controlled revelation is an accepted public-experience principle. The site need
not expose an immature real Phoenix merely because a technical capability
exists. It MAY use cinematic presentation, authored performances, conceptual
visualization, selected real demonstrations, future scenarios, mystery, and
bounded interaction so visitors can imagine something larger than the direct
demonstration.

Selective presentation MUST NOT become a false current-capability claim. The
public content model must maintain a visible semantic distinction between:

- **Now:** real live, implemented, improved, experimental/prototype, in
  development, blocked, demonstrated, verified, and promoted/unpromoted state,
  using existing canonical maturity meanings where applicable.
- **Vision:** approved direction, planned possibility, and long-term intended
  experience not represented as current production capability.

```text
vision != implementation
public demonstration != Phoenix capability
prototype != live
worker report != verified public claim
verified != promoted
internal evidence != approved publication
```

Fawkes is the first Phoenix being built and the primary real-world proof of the
project. Public evidence MAY include selected screenshots, embodiment and
development clips, demonstrations, milestones, comparisons, meaningful tests,
failures and fixes, or architecture summaries. Internal repository truth MUST
NOT publish automatically. An owning-domain evidence record, maturity state,
privacy review, and deliberate public editorial decision remain distinct.

## Semantic environment actions

The approved embodied-navigation direction is:

```text
visitor intent
-> installed semantic site action
-> deterministic validation
-> website executes the known action
-> companion performs the corresponding embodied behavior
```

Models and public content MUST NOT supply arbitrary JavaScript, DOM mutation,
selectors, URLs, or executable commands. A later implementation contract should
define installed semantic destinations, stable environmental anchors,
interruptibility, ordinary-link fallback, unavailable-action behavior,
accessibility, and reduced-motion equivalents.

Illustrative journeys include explaining Phoenix, showing real Fawkes evidence,
showing the future vision, opening technical depth, and explaining support.
These examples are not a frozen action schema or final information architecture.

This site-specific semantic-action boundary MAY later inform a more general
Presence/environment-navigation capability, but v0.1 MUST NOT claim or freeze a
universal Phoenix contract before another real manifestation requires it.

## Public and private isolation

Public infrastructure MUST be structurally unable to reach Tanner's Fawkes
Memory, Archive, Worker Exchange, private Development, repository, credentials,
provider routes, continuity state, or other private Phoenix data. Runtime
redaction is not an adequate primary boundary. The companion receives only an
intentionally public knowledge boundary and public-scoped services.

Anonymous public identity, session context, prompts, telemetry, model routes,
and actions MUST remain separate from future authenticated Phoenix state.
Payment handling SHOULD use an isolated hosted boundary so Phoenix
infrastructure does not receive raw card data unnecessarily.

The public experience must remain useful when 3D, model inference, audio,
payment, or advanced interaction is unavailable. Meaningful semantic content
and ordinary navigation precede enhancement. Mobile, weak-device,
reduced-motion, assistive-technology, high-traffic, and service-failure modes
require deliberate experiences rather than accidental degradation.

## Support and commercialization direction

The early commercial objective is to help fund development of Fawkes and
Phoenix. Public v0.1 SHOULD offer simple optional one-time support through an
isolated hosted payment boundary. It MUST NOT automatically promise Alpha or
product access, an account, features, release dates, lifetime plans, equity,
financial return, or other future consideration Tanner has not explicitly
approved.

Before support activation, the responsible legal/entity, transaction language,
tax and accounting handling, receipts, privacy, refunds/chargebacks, contact
path, geographic/age scope where applicable, and payment/provider boundary must
receive appropriate specialist review. This direction is not a legal conclusion
or authorization to accept funds today.

The companion may explain or navigate toward support after visitor interest. It
MUST NOT guilt, pressure, simulate emotional dependency, obscure optionality, or
initiate a payment decision through character performance.

Other future revenue paths remain possible under Foundation commercial
optionality. Side revenue SHOULD NOT materially delay Phoenix unless Tanner
explicitly accepts the tradeoff. Prefer work that funds or matures capabilities
Phoenix already needs.

## Animation and external-spend discipline

Some paid professional character-animation help is likely to be useful after a
representative rig/export/runtime boundary is stable. The working assumption is
surgical assistance—such as one excellent hero/signature performance and
possibly a few high-value revisions—not a large studio, agency, or commissioned
animation library.

The current total external pre-launch website budget is approximately **$1,000
maximum unless Tanner explicitly changes it**. This is a ceiling, not a target.
There is no current plan to hire a web art director. The project SHOULD:

```text
build for $0 first
-> identify an observed quality gap
-> pay only where specialist craft creates a large visible improvement
```

Blender (MCP) remains the long-term embodiment/technical-art specialist:
integrating and validating paid animation, maintaining rig/export compatibility,
layering procedural behavior, adapting variations, and preserving performance
through later embodiment changes. Paid work grants no embodiment promotion or
canonical Fawkes status by itself.

## Durable progression

The public home must support several distinct future public moments without
pretending they already exist:

```text
public website and purpose-built companion
-> selected real Fawkes demonstrations / "Fawkes is alive"
-> Phoenix Alpha
-> authenticated Phoenix web client
-> a rider's Phoenix manifests through the same public home
```

The anonymous guide MUST NOT morph into or be reclassified as an authenticated
Phoenix. When real web access exists, the public guide should visibly yield to
the separately authenticated, instance-resolved Phoenix manifestation. The
same public home may present both experiences while retaining independent
identity, authority, storage, deployment, and trust boundaries.

Alpha remains a separate product/commercial event. The first public website
does not promise or require it.

## Audience and durable distribution

The public home should remain the final destination for short-form video,
Reddit, technical audiences, press, partners, supporters, and future customers.
It SHOULD preserve stable deep links, shareable media, proof destinations,
campaign-safe routing, compelling mobile arrival, and high-traffic degradation
without fragmenting into separate audience websites or building social-media
infrastructure prematurely.

## Product guardrail

The public companion is a selling and learning instrument for Phoenix, not a
substitute product unless Tanner makes that strategy decision explicitly.
Website work is valuable when it funds Fawkes, produces reusable Presence/web
technology, improves public explanation, creates audience and demand evidence,
or matures manifestation quality. It is harmful when it substantially delays a
living Fawkes, becomes a pseudo-Phoenix product, consumes unbounded animation or
content resources, or redirects the Phoenix roadmap toward engagement and
virality.

## Maturity and deferred contracts

This document canonicalizes accepted product direction only. Website product
definition, visual design, companion prototype, implementation, support
activation, launch, and authenticated Phoenix manifestation are **not started**
unless separately evidenced and authorized.

A detailed Site Companion implementation contract is deliberately deferred
until definition and a disposable risk prototype establish real requirements.
Likewise, no separate Funding/Commercialization implementation contract is
warranted until entity, legal, payment, refund, and operational choices require
one. No framework, hosting vendor, payment vendor, model provider, rendering
engine, final name, exact behavior list, or final copy is selected here.
