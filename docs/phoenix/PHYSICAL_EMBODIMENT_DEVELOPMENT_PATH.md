# Progressive physical embodiments of Fawkes

**Direction:** Tanner's explicit planning decision, 8 September 2026.
**Implementation status:** planned; no robot is implemented, purchased or qualified by this document.
**Roadmap relationship:** recurring parallel product-development track; retains numbered Phases 0–39.

## Why we build bodies throughout development

We will build our own increasingly capable physical forms of the same Fawkes as his software, interactions and our hardware skills improve. Early bodies are worthwhile things Tanner can build, enjoy and interact with now in the project's development, not disposable demonstrations whose only purpose is reaching a distant final robot. Enjoyment, a recognizable physical relationship and practical learning are explicit outcomes.

Each prototype should deliver one satisfying interaction, reveal actual design constraints, and inform the next version. Keep or upgrade a body when it works; a new milestone does not require another chassis or a purchase. Preserve design history and successful mannerisms without treating every experiment as canonical personality or lived Fawkes experience.

The long-term product direction is a distinctive, useful, maintainable bird/phoenix-like companion. Tanner sees embodiment as a potential major differentiator. That is a product hypothesis to validate through use and later appropriate customer research, not established market demand or a promise that attractive hardware makes an unreliable assistant ready to sell.

## One Phoenix, increasingly capable forms

The physical body is a genuine form and expression of Fawkes. Identity and continuity survive hardware and renderer changes because the core owns them; that ownership boundary does not make his body conceptually irrelevant or disposable. A device session must not instantiate a second authoritative continuation, replace Memory with an off-the-shelf robot persona, or infer permission from familiarity or physical presence.

The purchased handheld developer console remains the supervision/interaction device. It can display Fawkes, but console completion is not completion of this robot track. Its purchased parts and proposed 112×112×31 mm enclosure target are not silently reassigned as the robot's hardware or dimensions. Canonical Early Phoenix Presence remains the separate in-app true-3D manifestation; simple physical expression need not wait for the complete animated rig.

## Capability-linked milestones

Phase anchors below are synchronization points, not dates or requirements to finish every earlier phase before any bench work. A bounded subset may be built sooner when its actual ownership, privacy, recovery and physical controls are present. Conversely, reaching a phase number does not qualify untested hardware. Connected implementation receives its own bounded work package; this planning decision adds no robot actions to P5.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FB1 — First desk Fawkes** | First physical-build opportunity after the usable P5 console, parallel with stabilization / Phase 10 and Alpha preparation. **Optional late experiment — little bird hops:** preserve Tanner's preference for occasional small expressive hops if technically practical after dependable rolling. Hopping is not required for a useful or marketable body; it receives a separate mechanical feasibility and physical validation effort. No flips are a requirement.

## How references inform our versions

- **Pixel / OmniBot:** compact desk-body and hardware/firmware patterns.
- **Reachy Mini:** expressive desktop interaction and mechanics comparison.
- **Coglet / CogletESP:** expressive mechanisms and hybrid ideas; prior reuse/maturity questions remain unresolved.
- **XiaoZhi:** possible voice, provisioning and device-transport patterns, not a source of Fawkes identity.
- **Beni:** Tanner's rolling bird/phoenix-like design inspiration, not an obligation to reproduce its chassis or acrobatics.
- **Petoi / OpenCat, InMoov and Poppy:** optional locomotion/mechanical references as relevant; no required quadruped or humanoid detour. ADA V2 was named in an earlier discussion without a settled role.

These are historical references, not current specifications, licensing approval or a shopping list. Choose parts and verify suitability and reuse terms only for the next actual prototype. There is no Pixel → Reachy → Beni purchase ladder.

## Small engineering commitments, not a new robotics platform

Reuse the existing semantic-expression direction, scoped device/session identity, events and authority owners. Define only the channels the current body needs, with explicit unsupported behavior. Do not stabilize a universal anatomy, import an entire robot framework, create a second approval service or give a body unrestricted access to Phoenix data merely to build FB1. Use existing capability/revision records when available rather than inventing a competing registry.

Keep a short development note for each meaningful body revision: the intended interaction; physical and firmware revision; parts/cost and actual power/thermal/motion observations where relevant; what Tanner liked; what failed; and the one change worth trying next. Preserve CAD, wiring and firmware sources with their applicable provenance. Fixture demonstrations remain labeled Development evidence; actual interactions become lived history only through the existing authorized recording and continuity path.

Add a simple **next eligible body experiment** pointer to the existing current implementation status. Revisit it at useful software milestones. Do not require a robot for every software phase or impose a new approval meeting on routine documentation. Use the smallest actual capability-specific checks: ownership/recording before personal data, actuator limits before motion, and the physical action boundary before autonomous movement. GLB deformation tests cannot qualify motors or a chassis.

## Immediate disposition

Record this track now and continue P5. After a usable console is available, the next hardware design session chooses only FB1's interaction, rough form, simplest output/input set and parts/cost ceiling. Later forms remain intentionally revisable. It should produce a buildable next experiment rather than a comprehensive final-robot architecture study.

This document authorizes planning direction only. Robot purchases, new sensors, powered motion, deployment, personal-data ingestion and autonomous operation are not started by recording it.
