# Console observation, identities and clocks

These views are observations, not a new campaign owner, scheduler or approval authority.

The current objective is the latest created canonical campaign, following a known
explicit parent link. Updating or expiring an older trial does not make it the
current objective. Campaign selection can inspect history explicitly. Summary and
its top-right update action continue to identify the current objective. An export
retains its exact selected campaign, source-record identities and saved content;
retrying its key cannot move it to another campaign. Copy uses the clipboard of
the browser's device, not a remote Windows clipboard.

Native execution clocks begin only at the actual app-server turn/start response.
Thread, turn, invocation, campaign and role identities accompany observations.
Permission waits pause active time; returning a decision resumes the same turn
until a real terminal event. Worker and Reviewer turns are separate, including
the adapter's existing fresh-transport structured repair. Queued review is not a
started Reviewer. Historical runs without these observations show unknown native
turn timing, even when their coarser stage intervals are available.

Authenticated state synchronization checks the recorded local process identity.
A healthy quiet process does not become stale merely because commentary stopped.
Client extrapolation stops after 30 seconds without a successful sync. A process
that disappears without a terminal event has unknown final active duration; no
unobserved interval is added. Completed durations never restart on refresh.
Bounded history (256 transitions per invocation, 32 projected turns) explicitly
marks incompleteness; an incomplete cumulative total is unknown. Elapsed clocks
do not alter any accounting limit or grant.

Combined alone adds observational flow participants under the existing component
identities. Server routes in src/app/server.py own authenticated projections,
exact Attention decisions and append-only update saves. The managed campaign
owner and app-server transport publish Worker observations and exact responses;
wsl_codex_reviewer.py returns independently validated read-only reviews. Current
and Roadmap retain their existing presentation and inventory.

## Timing correction evidence

A transport closure is a loss of observation, not a native turn-completion
timestamp. Its detection time never supplies a final duration. Previously
observed transition segments remain available, but the final active/waiting
totals remain unknown without a reliable terminal boundary. Likewise, denying
an action resolves the permission wait; the native turn may continue and ends
only at its own recorded terminal event. The action's denied outcome is retained
separately from the execution state.
An automatic read-only policy denial does not enter a Tanner-wait segment.

All history caps propagate incomplete-history information: 256 timing events
per invocation, eight projected invocations, 16 observations processed by the
clock helper, and 32 displayed turns. Retained individual durations remain
useful, but a truncated history never supplies an apparently complete role total.
