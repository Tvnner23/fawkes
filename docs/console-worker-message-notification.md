# Same-Worker final-message notification

This follows the accepted objective-outcome notification. A completed execution
turn is not a whole-task closeout. The existing canonical completion/decision
rules remain authoritative and unchanged; ordinary replies never renew a grant.

The existing authenticated `/api/development/worker` connection is also observed
on non-Worker pages, at most once per ten seconds. Hidden pages do not traverse
older history. The installed owner supplies the latest complete public final
reply with completed-turn evidence. The alert retains its full text, exact thread,
turn, message and content digest. No private reasoning or tool bodies are included.
Its timestamp says **verified**, not an invented message creation time.

The first successful synchronization establishes a durable baseline, not a new
completion. Later distinct completed replies may show **Worker replied**, with
the complete corresponding message in the scrollable dialog. **Reply to Worker**
opens the existing composer for this same conversation; it sends nothing. Whole
task completion still requires its separate canonical closeout and required gates.

Acknowledgment suppresses a duplicate alert; it does not dismiss a message being
read. Unchanged polls retain the open reply, and later replies wait until it is
closed. The latest corresponding reply remains manually retrievable after reload,
even when an older campaign blocker is still recorded. Failed hidden-page head
requests do not continue into history traversal or pending-reply reconciliation.

Baseline and acknowledgments use local storage with write/read-back. Repeated
head observations and reloads do not repeat acknowledged notices. Storage failure
never produces an automatic modal; the manual current-status/Worker entry remains.
Connection loss/overdue verification leaves last-known information, not a fresh
completion. A new conversation cannot inherit the old thread's alert baseline.

Native and managed permissions take priority. Typing, the touchscreen keyboard,
the menu, and other dialogs are not displaced. Ordinary final replies remain
quiet while Matrix is active; they may open after touch wake. The existing
permission wake behavior is unchanged. Acknowledging an old campaign blocker
does not permanently suppress a later public reply.

Qualification is synthetic until the installed Pi observes an ordinary real
Worker completion/decision. No extra model trial or simulated approval is used.
This change does not alter shutdown, clipboard delivery, native decisions,
campaign authority, touch calibration, startup, or the retained f68b48f rollback.
